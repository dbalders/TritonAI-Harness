# Ideas

**Ideas**, the lightbulb at the bottom of the sidebar next to Usage, suggests things TritonAI can do for you next. You can also open it from the command palette.

Ideas are listed in two groups. **For you** has ideas from your own work and tools:

- recent threads, and open loops in your [Memory](./memory.md) notes when Memory is on
- documents and data files you changed recently in your project folders
- plugins you have turned on and skills in the TritonAI catalog

**Things to try** has plugins and skills that appeared since you started using Ideas, and ready-made ideas that need no project, plus ideas for plugins you have connected, such as a digest of your recent email. A new installation starts with these.

**New features to try**, above both groups, lists TritonAI features you haven't used yet: helper agents that work side by side, threads TritonAI starts for you, scheduled tasks, and charts in replies. It starts collapsed, so select it to see the features. **Try it** puts a ready-made example of the feature in a new draft. A feature leaves the list once you send its draft or use that feature anywhere in TritonAI, and the list disappears when there is nothing left to try. Dismissing a feature hides it for good.

Each idea is one row: the task, one line about it, and what it is based on. Ideas never cite a thread, note, or file that does not exist, and every idea is ready to send as written, with nothing to fill in. To keep the page varied, no more than two ideas at a time are about the same project or tool. Ideas based on your work or tools are replaced after about three days, since the work moves on.

## Use an idea

Point at an idea (or tab to it) to show its buttons. **Open draft** puts the idea's message in a new thread's composer, or **Continue in thread** puts it in the thread the idea is about. Nothing is sent until you send it, so you can change anything first. The idea stays on the page, marked **Draft open**, until you send the message.

## Shape what you see

The **⋯** menu on each idea has:

- **Dismiss** hides an idea. It can come back after about two weeks if it still fits.
- **Snooze** hides it until the time you choose. Snoozed ideas are listed at the bottom of the page, where **Unsnooze** brings one back early.
- **Less like this** hides it and shows fewer ideas of that kind.
- **Not relevant** hides it for good.

Each of these offers **Undo** for a few seconds. Ideas you use, open, or push away also change the mix of what you see next, while always leaving room for something new to try.

**Suggest more**, at the top of the page, adds a few ideas right away. When there is not enough of your own work to go on, or new suggestions can't be generated, it adds starter ideas instead.

## How ideas are made

Ideas are written by your text generation model under **Settings > General > Text generation**, which must be a Codex model. If you have opened Ideas in the past two days, new ones are added in the background every few hours when there is new activity, up to 20 model requests a day. If it has been longer, opening Ideas checks for new ones then. Ideas are kept on this computer.
